  three-way evaluation.

- live plots with old smoothing, T1 metrics, hardware comparisons, or failed
  DCachehooping runs: superseded by the canonical refresh command.

To permanently reclaim disk space, review this archive after the active run
finishes. No deletion is performed by the organization script.
